Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

This app respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

Local Data Storage

All photos and videos stored within the app remain entirely on your device. This data is never uploaded, transmitted, or accessed by the developer.

Internet Usage

The app does not require an internet connection and does not communicate with external servers aside from the in-app purchase which only communicates with Apple's server.

In-App Purchases

The app may offer purchases processed through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled directly by Apple. The developer does not receive or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking tools.

Your Control

You have full control over your data. Deleting the app will remove all app-stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
